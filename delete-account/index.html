Delete your account

In the app: Settings > Account & cloud > Delete account. If you no longer have the app, sign in below instead.

This permanently deletes your Grindset account and everything synced to it: rules, focus history and usage from every device. It happens immediately and cannot be undone. Nothing is kept afterwards.

If you never created an account, Grindset only ever stored your data on your own devices. Uninstalling the app or using Reset this device in Settings removes it.

Your email and password go only to Grindset's sign-in server. This page has no analytics, cookies or storage.
