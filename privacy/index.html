Grindset

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the Grindset apps for Android and Windows and the Grindset browser extension.

The short version

  • Grindset works without an account. Without one, everything stays on your device.
  • It records which app or website was in use and for how long. It never records what is on your screen, what you type, page titles or full web addresses.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

What Grindset stores on your device

When you turn on activity tracking, Grindset stores the identifier of the app in use (for example com.google.android.youtube) or the website's domain (for example youtube.com), with the times it was in use. It also stores your rules, focus and break history, and settings. This lets it show your activity, enforce limits and block distractions.

Android permissions

  • Usage access: to know which app is in front, so rules and limits can apply.
  • Display over other apps: to cover a blocked app with Grindset's block screen.
  • Accessibility (optional, Google Play edition only): called "instant blocking". If you turn it on, Android tells Grindset the moment a different app opens, so a blocked app is covered at once instead of a second or two later. Grindset only reads the name of the app that opened. It does not read screen content, see what you type, or tap or change anything on your behalf. This information stays on your device and is used only for blocking. You can turn it off any time in Android's Accessibility settings.
  • Notifications and alarms: to tell you when a focus session or break ends.

Optional account and sync

If you create an account, Grindset stores your email address and a password (handled by the sign-in service, never visible to Grindset) and syncs, between your devices: rules, focus and break history, the app or website identifiers with usage times, your time zone, and a device label such as "Android device". This is stored with Supabase, in Sydney, Australia, and sent encrypted. Only your account can read it.

AI unlock check (optional)

If you turn on Settings > Ask AI first, then each time you unlock something while blocking is on, Grindset sends the reason you typed to DeepSeek, an AI service in China, so it can decide whether the unlock goes ahead. With it go what you are unlocking (for example "take a 15-minute break"), why it is blocked (for example "during Focus hours"), your local time and how many times you unlocked today. Your account email, activity and rules are not sent. Grindset does not store your reasons on its server; it keeps only the time of each check and whether it was allowed, to limit how many checks an account can make. DeepSeek's privacy policy covers what it receives. Turn the switch off and nothing is sent.

Website logos

To show a website's logo in your rules, Grindset asks that website directly for its icon, like a browser visiting its home page. The request carries no account or activity data. No third-party icon service is used.

Deleting your data

  • Account: Settings > Account & cloud > Delete account, or delete it on the web. Your account and all synced data are deleted immediately.
  • This device: Settings > Reset this device, or uninstall the app.
  • Export: Settings > Export data saves a copy for you first, if you want one.

Children

Grindset is not directed at children under 13.
